# Short Drama Studio

Make unlimited vertical short dramas (TikTok / YouTube Shorts) on your phone.

- AI writes each episode (Gemini free tier), continuing the series story.
- Locked cast: every character keeps the same picture and voice in every episode.
- Voices for each character plus a narrator (Gemini TTS).
- Animated scenes, bold captions, hook title, "to be continued" outro, 9:16 video up to 1 minute.
- Everything runs in the browser; your API key stays on your phone.

Open `index.html` in Safari, add your Gemini key in Settings, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
